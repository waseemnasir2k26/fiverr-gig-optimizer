# Fiverr gig rules used by this toolkit (checked October 2026)

Fiverr changes limits without announcement. Every number below says where it came from. If a number matters to you, confirm it in the gig editor before you rely on it. Update `fiverr_gig/rules.py` and open a PR if you find one has moved.

Legend: **official** = stated on a help.fiverr.com page or Fiverr-published page; **reported** = stated on a Fiverr community thread or third-party site, not an official page; **observed** = seen directly by the maintainers; **heuristic** = our rule of thumb, not a Fiverr rule.

## What the audit score means

The 0-100 score measures how well-formed a listing is against the limits and habits above. It does not know your category's competition, your reviews, or how Fiverr's ranking weights anything. A 100 will not rank you; a 40 will usually get you rejected or ignored for avoidable reasons.

## Things this toolkit will not do

- Log in to Fiverr or Upwork, scrape logged-in pages, or automate your account.
- Send messages or proposals for you.
- Help move a buyer off Fiverr. The message lint exists to catch accidental trigger words, not to disguise intent. Fiverr's terms keep communication and payment on the platform, and the penalty can be account removal.
- Quote your net as the sticker price. Fiverr keeps 20%, so to keep `N` you quote `N / 0.80`.
